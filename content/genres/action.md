
# Hi-Yah! Action! 
Action Games are, naturally, all about Action! While most genres of gaming include action, games in these genres focus heavily on it. Your goal is to shoot better, hit harder, live longer, and to do all these things more and more stylishly along the way. 

## Action-Adventure
![[gta.webp]]
> Games like GTA while still full of action, are perhaps more so host to fantastical (or modern) worlds full of places to see and things to do. While games like GTA try to recreate entire states with plenty of activities to do, others will create beautiful and vast fantasy worlds full of magical goodies to discover. The name of the game here is exploring and being rewarded for doing so! Whether it be a cool new weapon, a secret story to experience, or simply a funny moment to share. 

## Hack & Slash
![[phantom-blade-zero.jpg]]
>In Hack & Slash games, you're playing as a cool guy beating up bad guys in flashy and entertaining ways. Games like Phantom Blade Zero have done their best to make every sword swipe look as cool and stylish as possible, but they also have the distinct characteristic of the controls being rather simple. The amazing things your character does on screen are a result of you pressing square a bunch of times, and maybe a triangle at the end. 

## Character Action
![[dmc5.jpg]]
>Character Action games share plenty of characteristics with Hack & Slash games, and fans often get the two confused. One of the key differences between the two genres can be showcased by Devil May Cry, shown in the image above. Specifically the "Savage!" on screen, as character action games track all attacks you do, the order you do them, and how often you repeat those attacks and the order of them and give you a rating. Many find great enjoyment learning how to utilize every attack in the game to its most and get the highest rating they can, which in DMC's case is "Smokin' Sexy Style!". Another difference is how character action games have more intense controls that go beyond clicking the square and triangle buttons, making mastering them very satisfying!
