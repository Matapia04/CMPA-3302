# Think through your every move for these games! 

## What are Strategy Games? 
The ultimate brain workout! Strategy games come in a variety of IPs, Settings, and Gameplay styles! Although one of the more niche genres, Strategy games of all kinds provide a wealth of wonderful experiences and stories to play through. Whether it be through intense, real time decision making or a relaxing game of historical chess, you can't go wrong! 
## 4x Strategy 
![[Civ 7.jpg]]
>Explore, Expand, Exploit, Exterminate! 
>These are 4Xs that make up the pillars of the genre, whether it be through playing a historical playground like Civilization, making important decisions every turn to make your nation flourish and your enemies crumble, or through a much larger lens as you command an intergalactic empire in games like Stellaris, there's nothing more gratifying then watching your decisions result in a prospering people. 
## Tactics 
![[fire emblem.jpg]]
>The lives of your troops are in your hands in Tactics games like Fire Emblem, where you command a small group of units, usually 7-12, in missions that can shake the world, whether it be fantastical or gritty. While Fire Emblem is unique in that characters have deep stories and depth to their relationships, many Tactics games like X-COM allow you to create stories of their own as they pull through the smallest of odds to complete mission after mission, forging bonds with these hardy characters and inevitably suffering heartbreak when they fall in battle. 

## Real-Time Strategy 
![[starcraft 2.jpg]]
> Get ready to flex your fingers in RTS games, as they require you to make life or death decisions for entire armies in constantly changing battlefields! Whether it be in far off planets in Starcraft or in medieval fields in Total War, they demand strict attention of the movement of your armies, supply lines, and resource gathering through home bases.
