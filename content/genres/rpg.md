# Get your swords, potions, and maybe even guns ready! 
>RPGs, also known as "Role Playing Games", focus heavily on a world and narrative that center around your playable character, often allowing you to create a custom character of your own for the experience. Choices you make often have an impact on the characters around you and the story's narrative may remember it hours down the line or change entirely. RPGs aren't restricted to any setting or style of gameplay, you can play as a grizzled mercenary in a cyberpunk world in one game and as a master mage riding on horseback in another. Though in different regions of the world these games have become defined in their own unique ways, leading to "JRPGs" and "Western RPGs". 

## JRPGs
![[ff 10.jpg]]
> JRPGs are usually made in Japan, with RPGs from the country carrying many similarities across all the releases, gradually creating the label of "Japanese RPG". These games have centered around a group of characters coming together to save the world, with a heavier focus on the interpersonal relationships between the members of the group. While not always, JRPGs are famous for having combat take place during turns, and similarly JRPGs are more associated with fantastical worlds full of magic and whimsical creatures.  
## Western RPGs
![[skyrim 2.jpg]]
>In Western RPGs you'll find some common themes within the sub-genre relatively quickly, you'll often be prompted to create your character. Said character will more often then not be granted some unique power that makes them special, and be thrusted into the main plot, tasked with saving the world. The world in question is a massive sandbox where you get to go anywhere and do whatever you wish. Decisions you make will alter the world around you, saving a character can lead to a new plotline hours down the line as you run into them again, and they remember what you did and treat you far more warmly then if you ignored them. 
### CRPGs 
![[roguetrader.webp]]
>This sub-genre inside the sub-genre, a sub-sub genre if you will, derived from rpg games that were more granular then the traditional games, and used to be only available to play on computers. Hence the name, Computer RPGs! These types of games take much inspiration from table top board games, creating a character often comes with choosing stats like you would in D&D, and both combat and dialogue require you to roll D20s to see if you land a blow or pass a speech check. This is including all the previous hallmarks of the genre while being more tactical when it comes to combat.
