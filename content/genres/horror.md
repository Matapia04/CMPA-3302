# Get that Night light ready, because It's Spoooky Time! 

## About the spookiest corners of gaming

>Conceptually, It's rather obvious what horror games are; Games designed to scare you. But as the medium has gotten time to grow and evolve, the experiences these games provide has changed as well. However, the games within this genre have notably divided into 2 different styles of horror, originating from 2 different games that released in the 90s. Resident Evil, released March 22nd, 1996 pioneered Survival Horror, and Silent Hill, released February 23rd, 1999 pioneered the Psychological Horror Genre. But what's the difference? 

## Survival Horror
![[RE.webp]]
 > Resident Evil is renowned as "The Father of Survival Horror" because of a clear design focus shown by the developers at Capcom, who coined the phrase during marketing. Enemy encounters are tightly controlled, ammo for weapons is scarce, with no clear signs of knowing if those bullets you used can be replaced easily, and puzzles encouraging you to become intimately familiar with the environment as you travel to key places to pick things up or use said things to solve said puzzles and continue onwards. 
 > 
 > All of this blends into an incredibly tense experience providing scares and fun in abundance, with these core ideas inspiring countless other games to follow in their footsteps! 
## Psychological Horror 
![[Silent hill e.jpg]]
> Konami's Silent Hill took certain elements from Resident Evil, such as the puzzles and psychological aspects that were slightly present, and cranked them up to 11 while incorporating a story and gameplay considered to be a timeless classic. Silent Hill and its sequel are largely considered the pioneers of Psychological Horror, and can be attributed to two things: Sound and Story. 
> 
> While Resident Evil made you dread rounding some dimly lit corners for any number of reasons, in Silent Hill, Dread is omnipresent. Through the game's sound design and music, which ranges from haunting background to what can only be described as "metal pipes banging on trashcans and dragging across fences", your mind is never given any time to relax. An impressive feat considering all the games take place in the middle of a foggy day, said fog becoming more and more oppressive as you play.
> 
> The stories in the franchise also stood out at the time, and continue to strive to reach that standard with every new release. While many Horror games focus on external forces threatening you, much of the Horror is coming from the Main characters psyche: Trauma, Guilt, Repressed Memories, and more reflect into the world as grotesque monsters, twisted rooms, and more as said characters are forced to confront what they're hiding inside of them.
